## 🌐 About Travel Token Marketplace

The **Travel Token Marketplace (TTM)** bridges the gap between classic travel distribution and modern Web3 technology. By tokenizing traditional travel vouchers, loyalty rewards, and service vouchers into secure, wallet-ready digital assets, TTM enables travel providers to unlock new revenue streams, optimize distribution, and build direct relationships with travelers.

Through standard-based smart contracts, TTM automates the entire lifecycle of a travel token, from minting and distribution to real-time redemption and burning.

## 🎫 Products

- [Multi Card](https://www.travel-token.net/en/products/multi-card): a digital Wallet Card that bundles multiple benefits and services on a single card, available in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
- [Loyalty Card](https://www.travel-token.net/en/products/loyalty-card): a digital customer card for loyalty programmes and long-term customer retention, available directly in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
- [Hospitality Card](https://www.travel-token.net/en/products/hospitality-card): a digital guest card for hotels, resorts, restaurants and other hospitality offerings that makes information, services and added value available directly in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
- [AI Smart Token](https://www.travel-token.net/en/products/ai-smart-token): an AI-powered TTM Token that makes stored knowledge accessible through a web-based AI chat.
- [Travel Token Payment](https://www.travel-token.net/en/products/travel-token-payment): a payment layer that connects blockchain-based payments with the TTM platform and enables fast, transparent and automated payment flows between the parties involved.
- [Travel Token Marketplace](https://www.travel-token.net/en/products/travel-token-marketplace): a web-based, closed platform for digital services, additional services and partner offers.
- [Travel Token Messenger](https://www.travel-token.net/en/products/travel-token-messenger): a B2B transactional distribution layer for travel, where suppliers and distributors search, book and pay through one connection. It started as Camino Messenger, built by Chain4Travel on Camino Network, and TTM continues its development as multi-chain open source (starting with Base) under [LGPLv3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html). [Docs](https://traveltokenmarketplace.github.io/travel-token-messenger-docs/) · Code: [bot](https://github.com/TravelTokenMarketplace/travel-token-messenger-bot), [protocol](https://github.com/TravelTokenMarketplace/travel-token-messenger-protocol), [contracts](https://github.com/TravelTokenMarketplace/travel-token-messenger-contracts), [matrix app service](https://github.com/TravelTokenMarketplace/travel-token-matrix-app-service).
